# Overgrowth

A one-button climber where your failed runs become the tree you climb.

Built for Twigpine Project Treetop, Round 01 (Games), Oct 2026.

**Play:** https://cipoklean.eu.cc/overgrowth/

## How to play
- **Hold** to bend right, **let go** to bend left (mouse, touch, or Space).
- Your branch grows while it has **sap**. Hit a thorn, the edge, or run dry, and it hardens into wood.
- **Touch old wood to graft** onto it: +60 sap, so every run climbs higher than the last.
- Old wood darkens with age. After 4 runs it turns **brittle**: it holds one graft, then cracks.
- Cross a **canopy line** for +30 sap. Pass the third canopy and reach the **sunlight** to win.
- Then hit "New tree" and try to reach the sun in fewer runs.

## What's in the way
- **Thorns** end your run on contact.
- **Wind** bands push you sideways and bend your branch. Steer against them.
- **Frost** patches slow your growth, make steering slippery, and drain sap fast.
- **Beetles** show up from run 3 and chew gaps in your old wood. Touch one to squash it for +15 sap.
- **Dewdrops** give +25 sap and land in new spots every run.

Your tree is saved in your browser. "New tree" starts over.

## Run locally
Open `index.html`, or serve the folder with any static server. No build step, no dependencies.
